— OUR LEGACY

A Heritage of Academic Excellence

Shree Saraswati Secondary School stands proud as a pioneer of quality public education in the Satyawati Municipality of Gulmi. Founded in the year 2016 B.S. (1959 A.D.), our institution was born out of a collective communal desire to liberate children from the shackles of illiteracy and bring the light of learning to Satyawati-6 Johang.

Over the course of six decades, the school has undergone massive structural transformations. From a humble primary setup under thatched roofs, we have blossomed into a fully equipped secondary school, boasting a technical Computer Engineering stream, state-of-the-art information communication technology laboratories, smart digital boards, and robust library reserves.

We believe in education that is inclusive, practical, and highly integrated with vocational skillsets, preparing children to conquer regional and global opportunities alike.

  • Character First Principles
  • Inclusive & Public Focused
  • Modern Tech-Infused Curriculum
  • Dynamic Vocational Streams
👁️‍🗨️

Our Vision

To stand as a leading center of educational excellence in Nepal, cultivating technically competent, morally responsible, and socially dedicated citizens who are prepared to steer developmental actions locally and globally.

  • Achieving 100% digital literacy throughout the municipal zone.
  • Eliminating social disparity by providing free educational aids to all sectors.
  • Developing innovative research ecosystems in intermediate levels.
🎯

Our Mission

To impart holistic, modern, and value-guided education that blends scientific investigation, practical technical skills, and deep character foundations, supported by progressive instructional aids and highly trained faculty.

  • Fostering hands-on practical coding, computing, and electronic workflows.
  • Providing extensive scholarships, stipends, and supportive books to candidates.
  • Collaborating with municipal offices to build vocational training avenues.
— CHRONOLOGY & JOURNEY

Eras of Our Transformation

Explore the three monumental phases that shaped Shree Saraswati Secondary School from a rural basic classroom into Gulmi's leading model technical institution.

🌱
Phase I: The Genesis

Seeds of Literacy

Starting in 2016 B.S., our journey began under humble mud-and-thatch roofs. With zero resources but a resolute municipal drive, local volunteers gathered in Satyawati-6 Johang to combat child illiteracy, creating the first basic primary classrooms in the region.

🧱
Phase II: Structural Rise

Building Brick by Brick

Across the mid-2030s to 2050s B.S., the institution expanded to Lower Secondary and full High School tiers. Local villagers manually transported building materials across steep hills, constructing permanent brick halls and welcoming students from all surrounding wards.

⚡
Phase III: Digital Pioneer

Technological Model

Launched in 2076 B.S., the prestigious Computer Engineering stream (CTEVT track) marked our rise as a modern technical hub. By 2082 B.S., the school implemented complete digital pedagogy, high-speed labs, and interactive smart classroom networks.

— THE CHRONOLOGICAL RECORD

Historical Milestones

A detailed historical record of academic advancements, structural tier upgrades, and model upgrades in Satyawati, Gulmi.

🚩
2016 B.S. (1959 A.D.)

Foundation Inception

Established as a basic primary class on Saraswati Puja. Driven by rural community leaders, the school set out to serve Satyawati-6 Bedauri children under minimal thatch structures.

📈
2035 B.S. (1978 A.D.)

Lower Secondary Expansion

Officially upgraded to a Lower Secondary Tier (up to Class 7). Local families contributed building resources and manual labor to assemble permanent stone and timber classrooms.

🏛️
2045 B.S. (1988 A.D.)

High School Tier Upgrade

Reached Secondary School status. Initiated the first batch of Class 9 and 10 students, raising permanent brick campus chambers and preparing candidates for early SLC board exams.

🎉
2065 B.S. (2008 A.D.)

Golden Jubilee & Expansion

Celebrated 50 years of educational service! Constructed a spacious library wing, raised the multi-story administrative block, and established the primary science cabinet facilities.

💻
2076 B.S. (2019 A.D.)

Computer Engineering Stream

Introduced the CTEVT technical Computer Engineering stream (Classes 9 to 12). Set up a premium ICT testing lab, complete with networking systems and servers.

🚀
2082 B.S. (2025 A.D.)

Smart Classrooms Upgrade

Crowned as a Model Technical School. Installed high-tech interactive touch displays in all secondary classrooms, integrated digital libraries, and updated hardware testing arrays.
