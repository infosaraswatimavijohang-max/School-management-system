Welcome to Shree Saraswati Secondary School

Dear Students, Parents, and Well-wishers,

It is my absolute privilege and joy to welcome you to Shree Saraswati Secondary School, a center of educational excellence nestled in the gorgeous, peaceful hills of Satyawati-6, Johang, Gulmi. Since our historical establishment in 2016 B.S., we have committed ourselves to bringing standard, career-empowering education to the youth of our community.

"We do not merely teach curriculum; we spark curiosity, cultivate strong moral character, and inspire each student to realize their ultimate potential."

In this digital age, we have integrated modern pedagogical techniques alongside our values-based general studies. Our specialized Computer Engineering program (Grades 9 to 12) prepares students directly for tech-focused careers. We invite you to join us on this beautiful journey of knowledge, leadership, and success.

Chhabilal Bhandari

Head Teacher · Shree Saraswati Secondary School, Gulmi

✦ Est. 2016 B.S.

Empowering the Youth of Gulmi Since 2016

We started our journey way back in 2016 B.S. with a simple but powerful dream: to bring quality education to the children of Gulmi. Tucked away in the peaceful hills of Satyawati-6, Johang, our school has grown from a tiny local school into a vibrant home of learning that hundreds of local families trust every single day.

Here, we don't just focus on textbooks and exams. We believe in helping children grow into good, kind, and creative human beings who care about their community. Plus, to help our students match today's fast world, we also offer a special Computer Engineering course for Classes 9 to 12. We are proud of our roots, and we welcome every child with open arms to learn, laugh, and grow with us!

Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding our admission procedures, school timings, technical courses, and campus facilities.

How can my child secure admission into the Computer Engineering stream? ▼

Admissions into the specialized Computer Engineering program (Grades 9 to 12) are open to students who have successfully cleared the Class 8 evaluations. The selection is based on basic scientific and mathematical aptitude, evaluated through an interactive admission counseling session at our Bedauri Campus.

What are the school timings for the Johang and Bedauri campuses? ▼

Our standard classes run from 10:00 AM to 04:00 PM, Sunday through Friday. The specialized laboratory sessions and practice hours for Computer Engineering students can occasionally start early at 09:30 AM on designated weekdays.

Are there any scholarship provisions or fee exemptions available? ▼

Yes, as a public institution dedicated to social development, we provide complete tuition fee waivers and material scholarships to meritorious, economically disadvantaged, and marginalized students, guided by municipal education directives.

Does the school offer transportation facilities for far-away students? ▼

Currently, our students reside locally within the Satyawati-6 Johang and adjoining Gulmi communities, and commute on foot. We are actively discussing bus transit pathways with the local municipality for far-away student sectors.
